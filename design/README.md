## What to adapt
See design.md §12. In short: black / off-white / signal-yellow dominance, oversized numeric
hierarchy, rounded card language, floating pill nav with yellow selected state, circular
tinted category icons, paired-title weight contrast, alternating card stacks, donut / bar /
line chart primitives, hatched chart fills, starburst accents, restrained coral / mist /
mint surfaces.

## What not to copy
Specific layouts, branding, illustrations, exact dummy values, typos, or any typography
family that conflicts with design.md §4.

## Precedence
When an image and design.md disagree, design.md wins.
